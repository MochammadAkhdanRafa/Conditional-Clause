Conditional Clause

Pelajari 4 tipe kalimat pengandaian, lalu uji dengan 16 soal latihan.

Unless = if … not

Unless you enter the password, you cannot log in.
Sama dengan: If you do not enter the password, you cannot log in. Setelah unless, kalimatnya tidak memakai not lagi.

Kesalahan yang sering muncul

  • If it will rain, … → salah. Benar: If it rains, …
  • If I would have time, … → salah. Benar: If I had time, …
  • If I was you, … → di soal formal pakai were.
